1. Problem Statement:
         In many organizations, an employee's salary is influenced by various factors such as age, education, job role, years of experience, skills, and working hours. Analyzing these factors manually can be time-consuming and may not always provide consistent results. The problem is to develop an AIML-based system that analyzes employee-related data and predicts the employee's salary category as Low, Medium, or High.

2. Identify AIML:
        1. Domain: Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML).
        2. Machine Learning Type: Supervised Learning.
        3. Problem Type: Classification.
        4. Target Variable: Salary Category.

3. Define Objective:
        The main objective of this project is to develop a Machine Learning model that predicts an employee's salary category based on their personal, educational, and professional information.

    Objectives:
        1. To collect and preprocess employee data.
        2. To identify the factors that influence employee salary.
        3. To classify employees into Low, Medium, and High salary categories.
        4. To train and test different machine learning classification algorithms.
        5. To evaluate and compare the performance of the models.
        6. To select the best-performing model for salary category prediction.
        7. To predict the salary category of new employees based on their input data.
